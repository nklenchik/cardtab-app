Card Tab, in the logo’s style

Soft paper surfaces with a gentle top-to-bottom shade, rounded pill bars like the ones on the receipt, warm greys for anything secondary, and crimson saved for the one thing that matters. Round three: an Apple-style light background, white cards, barely-there shadows and lines. Only the look changes; every widget, size, interaction and color stays the same as the Card Board you have now.

Paper, not panelsCards are white paper with a hairline edge and a whisper of shadow.
Pills everywhereProgress bars are slim rounded pills; charts use clean bars with soft rounded ends.
Warm greysLabels, tracks and neutral bars use the receipt’s greys, so crimson stands out.
Receipt detailsDotted dividers and a torn edge on the entries list tie the app back to the icon.

Is it hard to change?

No rebuild needed. Colors, cards and shadows already run through a small set of shared settings, so the surfaces, greys and pills change in one place. The charts need a small update to draw pill bars with the two-tone top. The main thing to watch is speed on phones: soft shadows on many widgets cost a little, so they’d stay light.
